# Code of Conduct

[Chinese](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

This repository aims to provide a friendly, respectful, and safe collaboration environment for everyone. This policy applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, comments, code review, and any public communication related to the repository.

## Expected Behavior

- Use polite, professional language.
- Focus on the code, documentation, and issue at hand rather than the person.
- When discussing technical disagreements, provide verifiable facts, reproduction steps, and trade-offs.
- Be patient and respectful of different levels of experience during review and collaboration.
- When a problem appears, help with reproduction, diagnosis, and fixes rather than assigning blame.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Personal attacks, insults, harassment, discrimination, or threats.
- Malicious spam, deliberate disruption, or repeated derailment of discussion.
- Public disclosure of private information, secrets, credentials, or other sensitive data.
- Encouraging violence, hatred, or illegal activity in any form.
- Using authority, permissions, or social pressure to force others into inappropriate behavior.

## Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers should enforce this policy consistently, calmly, and fairly, while prioritizing the safety of affected people. When needed, maintainers may remove inappropriate content, limit interaction, or decline collaboration.

## Reporting

If you believe an interaction violates this policy, contact the maintainers through a private channel or GitHub's private reporting tools when available, and include as much of the following as possible:

- Relevant links or screenshots;
- When the incident occurred;
- The accounts or discussion context involved;
- The outcome you would like the maintainers to consider.

## Scope

This policy applies to public and semi-public interactions during collaboration on this repository. The maintainers reserve the right to interpret and enforce this policy based on the circumstances.
